Impact Study on Students' Attitudes and Opinions Regarding the Use of Artificial Intelligence

For Paris School of Economics, London Business School and the RAMD Fund (since March 2026). Three-phase impact study in Benin (baseline, implementation, endline). Programming of the baseline questionnaire, deployment on SurveyCTO, development of a dynamic real-time R Shiny dashboard for monitoring 30 data collection agents who administered more than 3,600 questionnaires; operational management of participants' use of the AskBenin and AskVera LLM-based chatbots; preparation and defense of the statistical visa file with INStaD.

Gender Inequalities in Bhutan using Civil Registration and Vital Statistics (CRVS-Bhutan)

For the National Statistics Bureau of Bhutan and the Ministry of Health of Bhutan, Aug. 2025 – Jan. 2026. Analysis of CRVS microdata (75,659 observations) and MICS/NBS data (26,314 observations): logit and OLS regressions in Stata, Oaxaca-Blinder decomposition to quantify the contribution of the child's sex to observed registration and mortality gaps.
