About Delphine

I trained in mathematics, economics and statistics at the African School of Economics and joined Development Expertise Lab in 2025 after a year at Inawa Institute. My work covers the whole life of a study: writing the questionnaire, training the field team, checking every batch of data as it arrives, then running the analysis and writing it up.

Outside project work I teach Python and Stata, at the AI Club CAEB in Porto-Novo and each summer at the Benin Excellence AI Summer School, and I am currently following the MITx / J-PAL MicroMasters in randomized evaluations and the BREAD-IGC course in political economy.
