Areas of Expertise

Fields of Work

My work sits at the intersection of field data collection, econometric analysis, and visualization, in support of applied research and evaluation projects.

01

Data Collection & Quality

Questionnaire programming (XLSForm, SurveyCTO, Survey Solutions, KoboCollect), high-frequency checks (HFC), back-checks, and cleaning of multi-country datasets.

02

Econometric Analysis

Logit and OLS regressions, Oaxaca-Blinder decompositions, and sub-group analyses on administrative and survey datasets of more than 75,000 observations.

03

Dashboards & Visualization

Development of dynamic dashboards in R Shiny and Excel for real-time monitoring of field agents — up to 30 agents and 3,600+ questionnaires.

04

Applied Machine Learning

Computer vision (YOLOv5, YOLOv8), time-series forecasting, and use of LLM-based conversational agents for applied research.
